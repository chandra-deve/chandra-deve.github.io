Multi-entity ServiceNow transformation

CAD $1.15M · ITAM, ITSM, HR Service Delivery
Problem
A multi-entity SaaS transformation across three workstreams (ITOM / CMDB, Service Catalog, HR Service Delivery) needed approved requirements, SOX-auditable traceability, and a working test model before the implementation partner could build. The access-request and product catalogs alone handle 5,000+ requests a year.
What I owned
The BRD end to end: 26 business requirements, use cases, NFRs, and security and data-classification requirements. The RTM of 83 requirements, mapped sprint by sprint to ServiceNow stories and Azure DevOps test cases. CMDB scope, locked with tower owners at 30 CI classes and 7 discovery sources. An analysis of 2,852 service requests across 61 applications to rationalize the catalogs. The ServiceNow-to-Azure DevOps test operating model: a 10-step sprint workflow and a 7-role RACI.
Result
The BRD cleared a six-director approval board across IT, Cyber, HR, and PMO. Every requirement is traced from design gate through UAT to transition to operations, and the test operating model was adopted by the Testing Center of Excellence.
